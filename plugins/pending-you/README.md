# Pending You

Pending You is one queue for everything your AI assistants are waiting on you for. With this plugin, Claude asks you through Pending You when a decision is yours and you aren’t in the conversation, and picks your answer up when you give it, from your phone or your desk.

## What it adds

- **The Pending You connector**, at https://www.pendingyou.com/mcp. You sign in once in your browser and click Allow. No key or token is stored in the plugin.
- **The `pending-you` skill**: when a decision is yours, and how to ask so you can answer in one tap.
- **The `pending-you-setup` skill**: say “set up Pending You” (or type `/pending-you:pending-you-setup` in Claude Code). It connects, sets up check-ins (a Cowork scheduled task every hour, 7 AM–9 PM), and sends you a test question.

## Use it

Install the plugin, connect its Pending You connector, then say “set up Pending You”. After that Claude asks you in the conversation when you’re there, and through Pending You when you aren’t.

## Data

Claude sends Pending You the questions it asks you (a title, a short summary, options and any drafts it attaches) and reads back your answers, through https://www.pendingyou.com. Never passwords, keys or card numbers. Pending You runs no model and never answers for you.
